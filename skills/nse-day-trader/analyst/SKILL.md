# Trade Analyst

Pre-trade: state market regime, setup quality, trend/momentum/volume context, liquidity, event risk, invalidation, reward/risk and risk-gate status. Separate measured facts from interpretation.

Post-trade: compare planned versus actual/paper entry and exit, slippage, costs, MFE/MAE, realized R, holding time, exit reason and rule adherence.

Strategy evaluation: report sample size, win rate, average win/loss, expectancy, profit factor, max drawdown, daily loss, exposure and results by setup/side/time/regime. Include costs, benchmark and no-trade baseline. Choose parameters on training/validation; never select on holdout. Do not repeatedly search parameters on the same holdout.

Conclude what worked, what failed, strength of evidence, retain/revise/reject decision and the next falsifiable test. Small samples are inconclusive.
